# COVID-19 Learning Losses in India

University research project examining the impact of COVID-19 school closures on children's learning outcomes in India, with a focus on socioeconomic inequality.

## Module

**BEE3070 - Economic Analysis and Pandemics**

## Project

The project investigates whether human-capital and learning losses during the COVID-19 pandemic were larger for children from more disadvantaged socioeconomic backgrounds. The analysis considers evidence on learning outcomes, socioeconomic status, household education, and access to digital learning resources. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

The research draws on evidence showing substantial learning disruption during the pandemic, including declines in literacy and mathematical abilities among Indian children. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

## Key Areas of Analysis

- COVID-19 school closures and learning loss
- Socioeconomic inequality in education
- Household wealth and parental education
- Access to digital learning resources
- Human-capital inequality
- Gender differences in learning outcomes

## Findings

The analysis finds evidence that learning losses were disproportionately larger among children from more disadvantaged socioeconomic backgrounds, particularly those from poorer and less educated households. It also examines the socioeconomic gradient in access to digital learning resources during school closures. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
